First time here? Set up Asterloom +
  1. Install the Mac preview. Download the ZIP from the v0.7.14 release, expand it, and open Asterloom. It supports macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. There is no Windows installer in this release.
  2. Connect AI for creation. In a new game, choose Connect AI to create. In Connect your AI, select an available provider, complete its connection flow, select an available model, then choose Back to my game. Provider charges or usage limits may apply.
  3. Keep this guide beside the desktop app. Download the sample source below, or expand its preview to copy it. The website provides instructions and materials; create and play inside Asterloom.

Finished games play without a live AI connection. AI chat with Aster is a separate, optional connection. These guides use the published preview’s controls; its gameplay is older than the development screenshot on the homepage.

AI creation time and the chosen activity vary with the provider and source. The sample checks help you inspect the result; they are not a guarantee of a particular game or learning outcome. The samples are original fictional material for personal and family learning under the Asterloom license.

One step at a time

Let’s try it.

Follow these steps in the desktop app. Mark them here as you go.

  1. 01

    Bring the handbook

    Copy the complete first-adventure.txt source below. In Asterloom, choose Create a game, then Paste notes. Enter Lantern Station Handbook as Source title, paste into Your material, and choose Add material.

    You’re ready when

    Lantern Station Handbook appears in Your materials with Ready beside it.

  2. 02

    Keep the choices simple

    Under Make it yours, choose Adults for Age group and Understand and remember for Learning goal. Open the source once to confirm the boat descriptions and worked dispatch are present, then choose Done.

    You’re ready when

    The readable material includes Swift, Shelter, Beacon, and the rule for a parcel with no route.

  3. 03

    Let your AI build

    Choose Connect AI to create, select your provider, finish the connection, and select an available model. Choose Back to my game, then Create game with AI. If your connection is already ready, go straight to Create game with AI. Wait for Your adventure is ready.

    You’re ready when

    The creation panel offers Play game. A saved source alone is not yet a finished AI-created game.

  4. 04

    Learn before the quest

    Choose Play game. Read the assignment, then choose Learn the ideas when offered. Read each short explanation before choosing Explore idea and Next idea. Follow the next-step control into the walkthrough or guided quest.

    You’re ready when

    You can explain why a destination match alone is not enough to dispatch a parcel.

  5. 05

    Make an evidence-based choice

    Try a mission using the rules you learned. For a 1-kilogram parcel to Reed Island that must stay dry, compare the cover as well as the destination and weight. Use the mission's feedback to revisit the relevant rule when needed.

    You’re ready when

    Your reasoning identifies Shelter because Swift lacks a cover, rather than simply choosing the larger boat.

  6. 06

    Check the material behind it

    Open Source materials and choose Download readable text. Compare the generated explanation with the handbook, then answer the source checks below. Keep the downloaded text as your reference.

    You’re ready when

    The original rule remains available independently of the generated lesson.

The payoff

What a good result looks like.

  • A new game appears in Your games with the handbook included.
  • You can distinguish a route's destination, capacity, and cover requirements.
  • You can open or download the material used to create the lesson.

AI chooses the activity and wording from the source. Your generated title, scenery, and mission controls may differ from another learner's game; this guide does not guarantee a particular template.

Make the learning visible

Can you explain it?

Try answering from memory, then open the source-based answer to compare.

Which boat should carry a 1-kilogram parcel for Reed Island if it must stay dry?

Shelter. It visits Reed Island, supports up to 5 kilograms, and has a waterproof cover. Swift lacks the required cover.

Which boat should carry an ordinary 2-kilogram parcel to Reed Island?

Swift. Both Swift and Shelter qualify, but Swift has the smallest sufficient weight limit. Exactly 2 kilograms is within Swift's limit.

What should happen to a parcel addressed to Mist Island?

Hold it for a supervisor. No listed boat visits Mist Island, so the source does not support assigning a route.

THE LANTERN STATION HANDBOOK
Original fictional learning material for Asterloom • NimeshBuilds

Your first shift

Lantern Station sends small parcels to three nearby islands. A good dispatcher does more than work quickly: they read the parcel record, choose a suitable route, and explain why it fits. This handbook teaches the station's rules. These are invented rules for a learning exercise, not shipping advice.

Every parcel record contains three fields: destination, weight, and whether the contents must stay dry. A destination is where a parcel should arrive. Weight tells you which boat can carry it. The dry marker tells you whether the parcel needs a covered boat. Read all three fields before choosing a route; a destination match alone is not enough.

Meet the boats

The Swift visits Reed Island. It carries parcels weighing at most 2 kilograms and has no cover. The Shelter also visits Reed Island. It carries parcels weighing at most 5 kilograms and has a waterproof cover. The Beacon visits Stone Island. It carries parcels weighing at most 4 kilograms and has a waterproof cover. No listed boat visits Mist Island.

The phrase “at most” includes the limit itself. A parcel weighing exactly 2 kilograms is within the Swift's weight limit. A parcel weighing 2.1 kilograms is not. A covered boat may carry either dry-marked or ordinary parcels. An uncovered boat may carry only ordinary parcels.

How to decide

First, keep only boats that visit the correct island. Next, remove any boat whose weight limit is too low. Finally, remove uncovered boats if the parcel has a dry marker. If several boats remain, choose the one with the smallest sufficient weight limit. If none remain, hold the parcel for a supervisor; do not invent a route or erase a requirement.

A worked dispatch

A parcel for Reed Island weighs 1 kilogram and has a dry marker. Swift matches the destination and weight, but fails the dry requirement. Shelter passes all three checks, so Shelter is the correct route. The explanation should mention the cover, because that is the fact that distinguishes the two boats.

Now consider an ordinary 2-kilogram parcel for Reed Island. Both boats meet the requirements, but Swift has the smaller sufficient limit. An ordinary 3-kilogram parcel for Stone Island belongs on Beacon. A parcel for Mist Island stays at the station because no listed boat serves that destination.

A useful habit

A confident explanation names both the decision and its evidence. “Shelter, because the parcel must stay dry” is more useful than “Shelter feels safer.” When a record is missing its weight, ask for that information before dispatching. Missing information is a reason to pause, not a reason to guess.

If you get stuck.

I cannot choose Create game with AI.

Confirm the handbook says Ready, then use Connect to select a usable provider and model. Choosing a provider without a model does not complete setup.

Creation needs attention instead of showing Play game.

Read the issue in the creation panel. Your source is saved. Restore the provider connection or model access before using the offered resume or retry control; do not repeatedly start new games.
