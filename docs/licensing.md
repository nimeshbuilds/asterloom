# Asterloom licensing decision

Asterloom's own code and original assets use the **Asterloom Personal Learning
License 1.0** in `LICENSE`. This implements the requested default: individual and
family learning is free; business and institutional uses require a separate
written agreement. The repository remains private until the owner decides to
publish it. Source-available licensing does not itself make a repository public.

Personal study for a job or certification remains free. Selling learning games,
paid instruction, client work, advertising-funded products, employer training
and organization-operated programs require commercial rights. Learners who
receive an appropriately commercially licensed game can play for personal
learning without buying a creator license. Original uploaded content stays its
owner's property; the embedded Asterloom runtime retains its software license.

## Options researched, 4 October 2026

- **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0** is a standardized source-available software
  license allowing changes and redistribution for noncommercial purposes. It
  also explicitly permits educational institutions and several other types of
  organization regardless of funding. That is broader than “personal learners
  only,” so it was not adopted unchanged.
  https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0
- PolyForm Strict omits modification and redistribution permission, conflicting
  with editable, shareable game exports. Shield/Perimeter restrict competition,
  rather than all commercial use. Small Business permits commercial businesses
  within its criteria. https://polyformproject.org/licenses
- OSI open source must allow commercial use. A license that reserves commercial
  use cannot accurately be called open source.
  https://opensource.org/definition-annotated

The selected license is a custom project license, not a PolyForm license or an
OSI-approved license. Have qualified counsel review its definitions, ownership,
consumer-law fit and separate commercial agreement before taking paid orders.
No payment checkout, price, jurisdiction-specific contract or technical license
activation has been claimed as complete. Restrictions are legal terms, not a
promise that a source-available offline program can technically prevent abuse.

Third-party components retain their own licenses. The included CC0 music remains
CC0; it is not converted to a personal-use license. Electron, libraries, fonts,
Tor and the local embedding model retain their separately supplied notices.
