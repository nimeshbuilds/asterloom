## Asterloom original interactive scores

- 90 original eight-bar arrangements: 75 individual game-recipe assignments and 15 additional focus moods.
- Prepared by `scripts/compose-interactive-scores.ts` and embedded as declarative note data in `src/data/interactiveScores.generated.json`. No samples, external melodies, model calls or remote music service are used.
- These arrangements share compositional grammar and six synthesized instrument palettes. They have distinct prepared melody/rhythm/harmony combinations; they are not marketed as unrelated studio recordings.
- Four layers (foundation, melody, detail and pulse) respond to reading, practice, exploration and feedback. Original acknowledgment cues are synthesized separately.
- Covered by the Asterloom Personal Learning License in the root LICENSE file. Included for permitted personal/family learning and free personal sharing; these original project assets are not labeled CC0. Third-party recordings retain their independent CC0 rights.

The two creators' primary upload pages and the CC0 deed were rechecked on 2026-10-04. No additional third-party tracks with uncertain licenses were included.
