Ten unmodified Ogg Vorbis recordings are included and embedded in offline exports.

- Pack: JRPG Music Pack #4 [Calm]
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/jrpg-pack-4-calm
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Tracks: A Place I Call Home; Childhood Friends; Peaceful Days; Sand Castles; Summer Memories; Innocence.
- The original INFO.txt is retained alongside this file.

## hernandack
- Pack: Short Loops Background Music Pack
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/short-loops-background-music-pack
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Tracks: A Brand New Wisdom; Just Saying Tho; Winter Dust; Swinging Sweet.
- The creator identifies the pack as composed entirely by hernandack and free to use in any project; credit is appreciated.

Licenses were checked on the creators' OpenGameArt upload pages on 2026-10-03. Composer names and recording titles remain visible in the studio. The ten original synthesized scores in src/lib/music.ts are fallback arrangements for browsers without Ogg Vorbis support, not the recordings' authorship.
