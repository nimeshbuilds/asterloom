First time here? Set up Asterloom +
  1. Install the Mac preview. Download the ZIP from the v0.7.14 release, expand it, and open Asterloom. It supports macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. There is no Windows installer in this release.
  2. Connect AI for creation. In a new game, choose Connect AI to create. In Connect your AI, select an available provider, complete its connection flow, select an available model, then choose Back to my game. Provider charges or usage limits may apply.
  3. Keep this guide beside the desktop app. Download the sample source below, or expand its preview to copy it. The website provides instructions and materials; create and play inside Asterloom.

Finished games play without a live AI connection. AI chat with Aster is a separate, optional connection. These guides use the published preview’s controls; its gameplay is older than the development screenshot on the homepage.

AI creation time and the chosen activity vary with the provider and source. The sample checks help you inspect the result; they are not a guarantee of a particular game or learning outcome. The samples are original fictional material for personal and family learning under the Asterloom license.

One step at a time

Let’s try it.

Follow these steps in the desktop app. Mark them here as you go.

  1. 01

    Start with Version 1 only

    Download both source files, but initially upload only two-sources-handbook.txt through Create a game → Upload files. Choose Adults and Investigate and explain.

    You’re ready when

    Your first game's material list contains the Version 1 handbook and does not yet contain the update.

  2. 02

    Create the original edition

    Connect your AI and model, then choose Create game with AI. Choose Play game when ready. Read the lesson and try a mission using Version 1's rules.

    You’re ready when

    You can identify the original standard-member limit of three books.

  3. 03

    Save before extending

    From the mission choose Save & return and wait for Saved on this device. On the game home, choose Edit materials, then Add sources & recreate.

    You’re ready when

    A separate draft opens with a notice identifying it as a new edition. The original has not been replaced.

  4. 04

    Add the scoped update

    In the new edition, choose Upload files and add two-sources-update.txt. Keep the original handbook too; the update depends on its unchanged rules.

    You’re ready when

    Both the handbook and Version 2 update appear in the new edition's source list.

  5. 05

    Build fresh from both sources

    Choose Create game with AI. This starts a new creation run using the included material. When ready, choose Play game and read the new teaching before attempting its missions.

    You’re ready when

    You can locate both the replacement rules and the rules that remain unchanged.

  6. 06

    Compare dates and scope

    Use the source checks below, or ask connected Aster to compare the two versions with citations. A map set checked out on 28 September keeps its original period; a new one checked out on 5 October uses the update.

    You’re ready when

    Your reasoning does not retroactively extend an existing loan or change a youth-member rule.

  7. 07

    Find both editions

    Choose Save & return, wait for the saved confirmation, then open Your games. Open the original and the new edition in turn and inspect their source lists.

    You’re ready when

    The original still has its first source and saved progress; the new edition has both sources and its own learning progress.

The payoff

What a good result looks like.

  • The original game remains separately available in Your games.
  • The new edition includes the handbook and its scoped update.
  • You can explain a changed rule, an unchanged rule, and the checkout-date exception.

A new edition is a fresh AI creation run, not an instant patch to the existing game. It uses provider requests again. Check that the generated teaching handles source versions correctly before relying on it.

Make the learning visible

Can you explain it?

Try answering from memory, then open the source-based answer to compare.

On 5 October, may a standard member who already has 3 books borrow one more?

Yes. Version 2 replaces the former 3-book limit with a 4-book limit on and after 1 October.

Does Version 2 change the youth member's book limit or allow youth map-set loans?

No. Youth members may still hold at most 2 books, and they still may not borrow map sets. Those Version 1 rules were not replaced.

What loan periods apply to map sets checked out on 28 September and 5 October?

The 28 September loan keeps its original 7-day period. The 5 October loan uses the new 10-day period. The update does not retroactively extend existing loans.

THE MAPLE LIBRARY LENDING HANDBOOK
Version 1 • Effective 1 September in this fictional exercise
Original fictional learning material for Asterloom • NimeshBuilds

Read a rule in its context

Maple Library lends illustrated books and map sets to its members. This handbook is a small policy-reading exercise. Its dates and rules are invented, and it is not the policy of any real library. A later, explicitly identified update may replace particular rules. Rules not replaced by an update remain in effect.

The original lending rules

A standard member may borrow at most 3 books at one time. Each book is due 14 days after checkout. A map set counts as a separate item, not as a book. A standard member may borrow at most 1 map set at one time, and that map set is due 7 days after checkout.

A youth member may borrow at most 2 books at one time. Each youth book is due 14 days after checkout. Youth members cannot borrow map sets. These limits describe the total items currently held, not the number of visits a member makes. Visiting twice in one day does not double the limit.

Renewal and availability

A book can be renewed once, adding 7 days to its due date, if no other member has reserved it. A second renewal is not allowed. Map sets cannot be renewed. A reserved book may still be returned normally; a reservation only prevents renewal under these rules.

Missing information

Before approving a new loan, the desk needs the member type, the type and number of items requested, and the items the member already holds. Before approving a renewal, it also needs the item's renewal count and reservation status. Without those facts, the desk cannot reliably apply the rule.

A worked example under Version 1

A standard member already has 2 books and asks for 1 more. The total would be 3, so the request fits the book limit. If that member asks for 2 more instead, the total would be 4 and would exceed the limit. The member's map set, if any, does not reduce the book limit because it has its own separate limit.

A youth member with 1 book may borrow 1 additional book. A youth member's request for a map set is not permitted. A standard member's unreserved book with no prior renewals may receive one 7-day renewal.

Reading multiple sources

When an update names a rule it replaces, use the replacement after its effective date. Do not mix old and new versions of that same rule. Keep unrelated rules, such as renewal conditions, unless the update changes them too. State the effective version when explaining a decision, so another reader can follow which rule you used.

The Maple Library Lending Update · Version 2

An explicit 1 October update changing exactly two rules and preserving the others. Add this source when rebuilding.

THE MAPLE LIBRARY LENDING UPDATE
Version 2 • Effective 1 October in this fictional exercise
Original fictional learning material for Asterloom • NimeshBuilds

The scope of this update

This document updates the Maple Library Lending Handbook, Version 1. It replaces exactly two rules from that handbook on and after 1 October: the number of books a standard member may hold, and the loan period for a map set. All other Version 1 rules remain in effect. These are fictional rules for practicing how to reconcile two sources.

The two replacements

First, a standard member may now borrow at most 4 books at one time. This replaces the former limit of 3 books. Second, a newly checked-out map set is now due 10 days after checkout. This replaces the former map-set loan period of 7 days. The new map-set period applies to map sets checked out on or after 1 October; existing loans keep their original due dates.

What stays the same

Standard books are still due 14 days after checkout. A standard member may still hold only 1 map set at a time. A map set still counts separately from books. Youth members may still hold at most 2 books and may not borrow map sets. A youth book still has a 14-day loan period.

The renewal rules are also unchanged. An unreserved book may be renewed once for an additional 7 days. A book already renewed once may not be renewed again. Map sets cannot be renewed, even though their initial loan period is now longer.

A worked comparison

On 5 October, a standard member already has 3 books and asks to borrow 1 more. Under the current rule, the total of 4 is allowed. If someone applies the old 3-book limit, their answer is out of date. If the same member already has 4 books, another book would bring the total to 5, so that request is not allowed.

A map set checked out on 5 October has a 10-day loan period. A map set checked out on 28 September keeps the 7-day period that applied at its checkout. The update does not retroactively extend that existing loan.

A reliable explanation

An answer about a current standard member's limit should identify Version 2's replacement rule. An answer about a youth member's limit may rely on Version 1, because that rule was not changed. A later document is not a reason to discard every earlier fact; compare its stated scope carefully.

If the question does not say when a map set was checked out, ask for the date before choosing between the 7-day and 10-day periods. The correct rule depends on that missing detail.

If you get stuck.

The finished game's upload controls are missing or locked.

Choose Edit materials, then Add sources & recreate. Finished-game sources are preserved; the new edition is where you add or change them.

The new lesson applies the update to every old rule.

Verify the update's scope against the source. It replaces only the standard book limit and the period for newly checked-out map sets. Keep the original edition as a reference and review the generated lesson.
