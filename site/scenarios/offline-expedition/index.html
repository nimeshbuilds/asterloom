What to look for

Verify that a finished desktop game keeps its material and saved progress locally, with adjustable music and feedback.

First time here? Set up Asterloom +
  1. Install the Mac preview. Download the ZIP from the v0.7.14 release, expand it, and open Asterloom. It supports macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. There is no Windows installer in this release.
  2. Connect AI for creation. In a new game, choose Connect AI to create. In Connect your AI, select an available provider, complete its connection flow, select an available model, then choose Back to my game. Provider charges or usage limits may apply.
  3. Keep this guide beside the desktop app. Download the sample source below, or expand its preview to copy it. The website provides instructions and materials; create and play inside Asterloom.

Finished games play without a live AI connection. AI chat with Aster is a separate, optional connection. These guides use the published preview’s controls; its gameplay is older than the development screenshot on the homepage.

AI creation time and the chosen activity vary with the provider and source. The sample checks help you inspect the result; they are not a guarantee of a particular game or learning outcome. The samples are original fictional material for personal and family learning under the Asterloom license.

One step at a time

Let’s try it.

Follow these steps in the desktop app. Mark them here as you go.

  1. 01

    Prepare a finished game

    For this source, download offline-expedition.txt, then choose Create a game → Upload files. Select Adults and Understand and remember, connect AI, and choose Create game with AI. You may instead reuse a finished game from an earlier guide to test the same offline workflow.

    You’re ready when

    The game has finished creation and offers Play game before you go offline.

  2. 02

    Choose your sound mix

    Choose Play game, then Enable game sound if sound is off. Open Adjust game audio. Enable Background score and Game & Aster sounds if you want both, then choose Follow this game. Set Music volume and Feedback volume, and try Play reward sound.

    You’re ready when

    You can hear the preview at a comfortable level or deliberately keep sound off. The preview does not award points.

  3. 03

    Learn and make progress

    Read the first lesson and enter its guided quest. Make a move or finish an activity. For the coastal source, use the observed signs and tie-breaking rules rather than assuming the same answer always applies.

    You’re ready when

    You have actual saved learning progress, not just a source waiting to be created.

  4. 04

    Save before closing

    Choose Save & return and wait for Saved on this device. If a save error appears, resolve it before quitting. Note the game's title and the progress shown, then quit Asterloom normally.

    You’re ready when

    The app confirms the save before you close it.

  5. 05

    Reopen without the network

    Temporarily disconnect your computer from the network, then reopen the same installed copy of Asterloom. Go to Your games, open the saved game, and choose Continue game when offered.

    You’re ready when

    The game and its sources are present without another creation request.

  6. 06

    Continue with regular Aster

    Continue the available lesson or mission. Open Aster and use regular Aster for What is next? or source lookup. Live online AI chat is not part of this offline check.

    You’re ready when

    Local guidance and the material remain available while you play.

  7. 07

    Finish the continuity check

    Use Save & return again and confirm the save. Restore your network whenever you are ready. If you used the coastal source, compare your reasoning with the source checks below.

    You’re ready when

    The new progress stays in the same local game; you did not need to regenerate it.

The payoff

What a good result looks like.

  • A completed desktop game reopens with its material and saved progress.
  • You can continue ordinary local play without a live online AI connection.
  • You can independently adjust music and feedback or choose silent play.

Creation must finish and saves must succeed before this test. Online AI answers, new model or runtime downloads, and remote services still need connectivity. Progress is local to this installation; it is not automatic cross-device sync. Source checks below apply only to the coastal kit.

THE THREE-STOP COASTAL SURVEY
Original fictional learning material for Asterloom • NimeshBuilds

Three investigations, one field record

The Coastal Survey is a fictional observation exercise with three stops: Pebble Gate, Mirror Pool, and Wind Post. Each stop teaches a different reasoning step. The investigator records the observation, its meaning under the local rule, and the next action. These rules belong to an invented game world, not a real coastal safety guide.

Stop one: Pebble Gate

Pebble Gate has three numbered channels. Channel 1 is marked open, Channel 2 is marked blocked, and Channel 3 is marked open. The survey cart must use the lowest-numbered open channel. “Lowest-numbered” is a tie-breaking rule: it matters only when more than one channel is open. A blocked channel must never be selected just because its number is small.

With the recorded signs, Channel 1 is correct. If Channel 1 becomes blocked while Channels 2 and 3 keep their recorded states, Channel 3 becomes correct. Record which signs you saw; the route depends on the current observation, not on a remembered answer from an earlier visit.

Stop two: Mirror Pool

Mirror Pool has a depth marker with green, amber, and red bands. Green means continue the survey. Amber means take a second reading. Red means stop this part of the survey and report the observation. An amber reading is uncertainty that needs another measurement; it is not permission to continue yet.

The second reading replaces the first for the immediate decision. If the first reading is amber and the second is green, continue and record both readings. If the second is amber again, report that the result remains uncertain. If the second is red, stop and report it. Do not keep taking readings until one happens to look favorable.

Stop three: Wind Post

Wind Post stores three flags with recorded counts: North has 4 marks, East has 7 marks, and West has 7 marks. Choose the flag with the greatest count. If counts tie, choose the tied flag that comes first alphabetically. East and West tie at 7, so East wins the tie-break. North does not enter the tie-break because its count is smaller.

A complete field record

For the original observations, a concise record says: “Pebble Gate: Channel 1, the lowest open channel. Mirror Pool: amber then green, so continue after the second reading. Wind Post: East, tied for the greatest count and first alphabetically among the tied flags.”

Keeping your place

After each stop, write which stop is complete and which observation supported its decision. A later investigator should be able to resume at the next unfinished stop without guessing. A recorded result is useful only when it preserves the relevant evidence; a bare “done” does not explain what happened or why.

If you get stuck.

My music repeats one track.

Open Adjust game audio and select Follow this game instead of Keep this track. A changing soundtrack still depends on the game's selected audio direction and available pieces.

I reopened the app but cannot find my game.

Check that you reopened the same Asterloom installation and local workspace, rather than a separate exported game. Your game library and progress are not automatically synchronized between copies.

Aster's online AI request fails while offline.

Use regular Aster for local source lookup and contextual guidance. Restore connectivity before requesting another answer from an online provider.
