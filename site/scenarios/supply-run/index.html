First time here? Set up Asterloom +
  1. Install the Mac preview. Download the ZIP from the v0.7.14 release, expand it, and open Asterloom. It supports macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. There is no Windows installer in this release.
  2. Connect AI for creation. In a new game, choose Connect AI to create. In Connect your AI, select an available provider, complete its connection flow, select an available model, then choose Back to my game. Provider charges or usage limits may apply.
  3. Keep this guide beside the desktop app. Download the sample source below, or expand its preview to copy it. The website provides instructions and materials; create and play inside Asterloom.

Finished games play without a live AI connection. AI chat with Aster is a separate, optional connection. These guides use the published preview’s controls; its gameplay is older than the development screenshot on the homepage.

AI creation time and the chosen activity vary with the provider and source. The sample checks help you inspect the result; they are not a guarantee of a particular game or learning outcome. The samples are original fictional material for personal and family learning under the Asterloom license.

The payoff

What a good result looks like.

  • You identify which requirements are mandatory before optimizing a bonus.
  • You check both coin and turn limits rather than only one.
  • You can justify the best supported plan using totals and rejected alternatives.

These are fictional tabletop rules, not real expedition advice. Asterloom may choose a decision or reasoning activity rather than a particular resource-management interface.

What does one water kit, one food kit, and one blanket kit cost in total?

9 coins and 5 turns. Those three kits completely supply both explorers, leaving 3 coins and 3 turns.

Which feasible bonus earns the most points after buying the essentials?

The signal earns 5 points. Including essentials, it costs 12 coins and 7 turns, staying within the limits of 12 coins and 8 turns.

Why can the team not add both field notes and marker repair after the essentials?

Those two bonuses together cost 4 coins, but only 3 coins remain. Their combined time fits the remaining 3 turns, but the coin limit still rules the pair out.

THE PINE TRAIL SUPPLY CHALLENGE
Original fictional learning material for Asterloom • NimeshBuilds

Plan within two limits

Pine Trail is a fictional tabletop expedition. Two explorers need supplies for a single round. The quartermaster has 12 coins and 8 turns. Every purchase consumes both coins and turns. A valid plan must stay within both limits and meet the essential supply requirements before considering bonus actions. These rules are a planning exercise, not advice for a real outdoor trip.

Essential supplies

Each of the two explorers needs one water unit, one food unit, and one blanket. A water kit contains two water units and costs 4 coins and 2 turns. A food kit contains two food units and costs 3 coins and 2 turns. A blanket kit contains two blankets and costs 2 coins and 1 turn. One of each kit therefore supplies both explorers completely.

Kits cannot be split into smaller purchases. Each listed kit or bonus action can be selected at most once in the round. Supplies cannot substitute for each other: an extra blanket does not replace a missing water unit. A plan missing an essential supply is incomplete, even if it earns many bonus points.

Bonus actions

After securing the essentials, the team may activate a signal for 3 coins and 2 turns, earning 5 bonus points. They may collect field notes for 2 coins and 1 turn, earning 4 bonus points. They may repair a trail marker for 2 coins and 2 turns, also earning 4 bonus points. Unspent coins and turns earn no bonus points. The round's objective is to complete the essentials and then earn the largest possible bonus within the limits.

A worked plan

The water, food, and blanket kits together cost 4 + 3 + 2 = 9 coins. They use 2 + 2 + 1 = 5 turns. That leaves 3 coins and 3 turns. Adding the signal costs the remaining 3 coins and another 2 turns. The final plan uses 12 coins and 7 turns, meets all essential requirements, and earns 5 bonus points.

Field notes would also fit, giving a total of 11 coins and 6 turns with 4 bonus points. The marker repair fits at 11 coins and 7 turns with 4 points. No two bonus actions fit the remaining 3 coins, since even the cheapest pair costs 4 coins. Therefore, the signal is the highest-scoring bonus for this round.

Why check both columns?

A plan can be affordable yet too slow, or quick enough yet too expensive. Add each column separately before choosing. State the total, the remaining resources, and whether every essential requirement is satisfied. This makes a decision explainable and exposes a hidden trade-off before the round begins.

If you get stuck.

The generated score differs from the source's five bonus points.

The game's practice or XP score is separate from the fictional round's bonus points. Check the supply plan against the source rules; do not treat the app's XP as an extra supply rule.

A plan fits the budget but feels wrong.

Check the turn total and every essential requirement too. The source requires all three conditions to hold.
