# Pilot audio

Three unmodified Ogg Vorbis recordings by **hernandack** are bundled locally:

- A Brand New Wisdom
- Just Saying Tho
- Winter Dust

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/short-loops-background-music-pack
License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The full CC0 legal text is in `licenses/CC0-1.0.txt`. These files are exact copies
of the already licensed Asterloom bundled recordings, with no transcoding.

The six event cues are original deterministic PCM synthesis in AsterloomAudio.gd,
not samples or generated imitations of these recordings. They remain under the
Asterloom project license; the independent third-party recordings remain CC0.

The pilot cycles the three recordings at their actual end. Scene selection changes
the initial playlist order and cue timbre/root. This is not three unique composed
soundtracks for every possible scaffold. No external service or AI is required.
